How It All Began

In 1976, Unilorin Christian Union began as a small, passionate gather of just a handful of students driven by a shared desire for spiritual growth, fellowship, and prayer on campus.

What started in quiet devotion has blossomed over 50 years into a vibrant interdenominational community spanning multiple centers across the university, nurturing generations of believers who lead with excellence across the world.
